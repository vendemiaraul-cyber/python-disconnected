A mini TikTok where you write the algorithm.

A phone-sized For You feed for your desktop. It downloads only a few videos at a time, shows up to 30 read-only comments you can export, and ranks every video with an algorithm you pick, tune, type yourself, or fit from your own TikTok data.

Small on purpose

The screen looks like TikTok, but the right edge has only two buttons: comments and export. No likes, no favourites, nothing to post.

▶

TikTok-style feed

Full-screen looping video, For You header, caption and sound at the bottom. Scroll, drag or use the arrow keys to move between videos.

💬

30 comments, read-only

Up to 30 comments are downloaded for the video on screen and deleted as soon as you swipe away.

➦

Export

Save the current video's comments as JSON, CSV or plain text.

⚙

Settings on the For You page

Pick a type of algorithm, move weight sliders, or type your own ranking formula.

◎

Learns from your data

Tap Continue with TikTok and your watch history arrives through TikTok's official API. MiniTok fits the algorithm by itself.

⌫

Light on storage

Only the previous, current and next couple of videos stay on disk. The cache is wiped on exit.

What it looks like

Screenshots of the desktop app on its offline demo clips. Or skip the install and try the browser preview: same screen, same algorithm, works on your phone.

These screenshots use the built-in offline demo, so the clips are test patterns and the comments are placeholder text, not real TikTok comments. In the desktop app, comments for TikTok videos are fetched live with Scrapy; that has been checked against real TikTok (30 of a video's 2,012 comments).

Choose, tune or write the algorithm

Every video gets nine signals between 0 and 1. The algorithm decides how much each one counts.

While you watch, finishing or looping a video boosts its tags, creator and sound, and skipping lowers them. Press I to see why a video was picked.

Find your algorithm from your own data

MiniTok fits the model that best explains what you finish, rewatch and skip. Everything stays on your computer.

  1. In MiniTok, open ⚙ › Find my algorithm and tap Continue with TikTok. Approve in your browser and keep watching: TikTok's official Data Portability API sends your history to MiniTok. Or load a "Download your data" file yourself.
  2. MiniTok reads watch history, likes, favourites, shares, searches and follows. It estimates watch time from the gaps between timestamps and looks up a sample of the videos.
  3. It fits a logistic regression and an interest profile, shows a report, and lets you apply the result.

TikTok's own recommender is private, so no app can copy it exactly. What this recovers is your personal ranking, as closely as your data allows.

Install

Needs Python 3.10+. The offline demo also needs ffmpeg.

# get the code
git clone -b claude/minitok-app \
  https://github.com/vendemiaraul-cyber/python-disconnected
cd python-disconnected
pip install -e .

# try it offline
minitok demo

# real TikTok creators and hashtags
minitok --source @nasa --source "#cooking"
